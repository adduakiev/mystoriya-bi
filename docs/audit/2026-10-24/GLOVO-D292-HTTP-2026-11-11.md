`GlovoSheetsTransport` contract. It requires explicit `enabled:true`, injected
fetch and an injected opaque bearer provider. Import/factory creation perform
zero I/O. There is no default fetch, credential/env discovery, key, OAuth exchange,
refresh, retry, public export fallback, logging or active runtime import.

| --- | --- |
