## Upgrade trigger

If Windsor exceeds 10 data sources:
- move Plus → Professional;
- expected incremental Windsor budget: approximately +$3k/year versus Plus;
- use only when required by actual connector count.

## Architecture principle

Use Windsor for marketing / advertising / social / merchant / attribution sources.

Prefer direct integrations for:
- Firebase when possible;
- PageSpeed when useful;
- backend orders / CRM;
- internal operational data.

This preserves Windsor source capacity and keeps core business data under our control.
